# Mark: service reminders and the office list

The system is designed to remind customers about their annual service or landlord certificate, offer real diary space and save an agreed visit. Sarah gets an urgent-first follow-up list, a summary and recording, yesterday’s bookings and campaign progress. Mark gets complaints. Only Dan and Priya can be assigned gas work.

It identifies itself as an AI assistant. It will not pressure someone who says no, take payment, invent a price, give discounts, call tenants or disclose an account to someone else. A permanent do-not-call request prevents future calls. Gas or carbon monoxide reports interrupt routine booking for safety advice. Genuine complaints and frustrated customers are heard; persistent unrelated or personal abuse receives a warning before a polite ending.

**What works today** — The office is deployed with personal logins and permissions. On 11 November an attended UK call saved a fictional £95 boiler service with Priya Shah, with the result, recording and transcript available in the office. Six roleplay records are indexed, including the three-property landlord booking and the saved opt-out.

**Before customer calling** — The office needs an approved callback route, a verified first voicemail message and a handset check of the spoken opt-out acknowledgement. The assessor’s live call and independent voice and response-time checks remain separate. Automatic customer calling is paused.
